About the conference

What is Biology27?

Biology27 aims to provide a platform for exchange between students, researchers and professors across scientific institutions, research groups and topic areas. Biology27 is also the annual joint meeting of the Swiss Zoological, Botanical, and Systematics societies.

Venue

vonRoll University Centre (highlighted in red), between Fabrikstrasse and the Länggasse quarter — official map, University of Bern

vonRoll University Centre, Building 6

Fabrikstrasse 6, 3012 Bern, Switzerland

The venue sits right next to the Güterbahnhof PostBus stop (line 101), a short ride from Bern main station, and a ~15-minute walk through the Länggasse quarter. Tram line 20 (Länggasse stop) also passes nearby.
